Privacy Policy for Fronimus

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fronimus collects and uses data.

Fronimus is developed by a solo developer. I use limited analytics to understand how players use the game, find bugs, see where players get stuck, and improve the game.

Analytics

Fronimus uses Unity Analytics.

If you allow analytics in the game, Fronimus may send anonymous gameplay and technical data, such as:

  • game sessions
  • game version
  • platform information
  • completed guidebook goals
  • gameplay events
  • machine/building events
  • error reports or technical events
  • general progress information

This data helps me understand what works, what breaks, and what should be improved.

Data I do not collect

I do not intentionally collect:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your personal messages
  • your payment information
  • your exact location
  • your passwords
  • your private files

Please do not send personal information through any in-game text fields, reports, or feedback forms.

Technical data collected by Unity Analytics

Unity Analytics may process technical identifiers and device/network information needed to provide analytics services. This may include data such as IP address, installation-specific identifiers, user identifiers, device/platform information, and similar technical data.

Unity processes this data according to its own privacy and data processing policies.

Consent

Fronimus asks for your permission before starting analytics data collection.

You can choose:

  • Allow Analytics - analytics will be enabled.
  • Decline - analytics will not be enabled.

You can still play Fronimus even if you decline analytics.

Changing your choice

You can disable analytics later in the game settings if this option is available.

If analytics is disabled, Fronimus will stop sending analytics data from that point forward.

Data deletion

If you want to request deletion of analytics data connected to your installation, you can contact me using the email below.

I will try to help with the request using the tools provided by Unity Analytics.

Third-party services

Fronimus may use the following third-party service:

Unity Analytics
Used for gameplay analytics, technical events, and understanding player behavior.

You can learn more by reading Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy on Unity's official website.

Children's privacy

Fronimus is not intended to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Fronimus, please contact me so I can review and handle the request.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If I make important changes, I will update the Last updated date at the top of this page.
